# Release audit for v0.3.1: a checker artifact

**Snapshot.** v0.3.0 (`bfd9986`), 20 Oct 2026.

**Context.** A Claude agent was writing reference designs for new tasks, outside this release, and found that some correct parts failed the checker. The same can happen to a model's file on any task, so every saved attempt was audited. Like the earlier audits, this was done by Claude, which also wrote the work, so it is not independent review.

## Cause

- OpenSCAD 2026.10.01 exports with its Manifold backend by default.
- Where two unioned parts meet flush, the export can leave zero-area sliver triangles. An example is a spout tube standing exactly on a cone's bottom ring.
- The mesh is then not watertight. `checks.py` counts dozens of separate bodies (49 and 113 in the cases found), and the volume is undefined.
- A correct part therefore fails its piece-count and volume checks. The measurement report sent to the model also says that the part is in dozens of pieces.
- The same file exported with `--backend CGAL` is watertight and in one piece.

## Fix

- `load_mesh` (`tools/scadreport.py`) now cleans any mesh that is not watertight before it is checked or reported. It merges vertices that round together at 1e-5 mm and drops zero-area faces.
- A watertight mesh is never touched. A genuinely open part, such as two cubes touching along an edge, stays open and still fails.
- New regression test in `bench/test_harness.py`: a tube standing flush on a cone's ring now reads as one watertight body. Without the fix it is 49 bodies with no volume.
- `bench/test_harness.py` (all pass) and `bench/build_refs.py` (references, invariance, discrimination, mutants and variants: all OK) were run with the fix.

## Audit

- **Method.** `bench/rescore.py --only-open --dry-run` was run over all 263 committed runs.
  - `--only-open` re-checks every attempt whose recorded mesh was not watertight. These are the only attempts the fix can change, since no attempt's mesh analysis had failed.
  - Record: [`2026-10-20_v0.3.1_rescore.txt`](2026-10-20_v0.3.1_rescore.txt).
- **Totals.** 4,227 conversations; 31 attempts change; no files missing; no re-render failed.
- **Three attempts now pass.** All three are Qwen3.5 9B funnels whose spout sits flush on the cone, each on its conversation's third attempt. Each conversation goes from fail to pass:
  - `gpu-base-g3`, sample 5, and `gpu-base-h0-g6`, sample 4: the base model on a rented GPU;
  - `main-tiiny`, sample 0: the NPU box.
- **The other 28 changes are scores of attempts that still fail.** 25 go up by one or two checks and 3 go down by one.
- **Unpublished runs.** A separate scratch audit covered every results folder, including runs not yet published. It re-rendered each open mesh and checked it before and after the clean-up. It found the same three passes and no others.
- **What the fix cannot undo.** The feedback inside the affected conversations was computed from the uncleaned mesh: where an earlier attempt's mesh had slivers, the model was told its part was in many pieces. Re-scoring cannot change what the model did next. This touches at most the 17 conversations whose scores changed, out of 4,227.

- **Table 11.** The stock base with h0: pass@8 in-family from 2/8 to 3/8, samples passed from 5/64 to 6/64, and passes won by repair from 0 to 1.
- **Tables 12 and 13 and Figures 5 and 6.** Qwen3.5 9B on the box passes 2 tasks in sample 0 instead of 1 (mean 1.0 instead of 0.5).
- **Text.**
  - The stock base passed four tasks in eight samples, not three. It won four passes by repair, not three, so v9 seed 1 and v11 won "one fewer than" the base where they had won "as many as" it.
  - The better prompt took the base from three tasks to six in eight samples, not from two.
