Looking at the specification and report carefully, I notice a potential issue with the wall height.

The specification states: "na unapanda kutoka kwenye bamba hadi z = 70" which means "and rises from the base until z = 70". 

Since the base ends at z=4, this means the walls should extend from z=4 to z=70, giving them a height of 66 mm, not 70 mm.

Currently, my model has the walls extending to z=74 (height of 70 mm). I need to correct this to make the walls reach z=70 (height of 66 mm).
