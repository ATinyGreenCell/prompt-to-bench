The specification requires the tank to be an "open-top" container. The current model renders as 3 separate bodies because the top face of the outer shell is missing (it's open), but the electrode holes are drilled through the side walls. This creates a situation where the "void" logic in the `difference()` operation is not correctly isolating the internal chambers from the external space, resulting in the side walls being disconnected from the main body or the holes creating separate floating islands.

Specifically, the electrode holes are drilled at `z=30`. The report shows holes at `z=30.50` (centered at 30, so top is 31, bottom is 29). The tank height is 40. The holes are correctly positioned relative to the tank height. However, the geometry construction is flawed. The `union()` of the void shapes and the `cube()` of the outer shell are not interacting correctly to form a single continuous shell with holes.

The correct approach for an open-top tank with holes in the side walls is to:
1.  Create the solid outer block.
2.  Subtract the inner void (which includes the two chambers and the space above the platform).
3.  Subtract the platform.
4.  Subtract the electrode holes.

The previous attempt failed because the void subtraction logic for the chambers was relative to the wrong origin or the union of the void didn't fully clear the path for the holes to connect the inside to the outside without breaking the shell integrity.

Let's rebuild using a simpler, more robust "shell subtraction" method.
1.  Outer block: 120x70x40, centered.
2.  Inner void: A single shape representing the empty space.
    *   The empty space is the union of:
        *   The two buffer chambers (left and right).
        *   The space above the platform.
    *   Crucially, the void must extend all the way to the top (Z=40) and to the outside of the walls (Y=-35 to 35, X=-60 to 60) to ensure the "open top" and "side holes" are part of the void, not the solid.
3.  Platform: Solid block inside.
4.  Holes: Cylinders subtracted from the solid.
